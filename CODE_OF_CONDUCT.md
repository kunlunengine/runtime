# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Kunlun Runtime is committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community. Everyone who
participates in good faith is entitled to respect, regardless of background, identity, experience,
language, ability, nationality, appearance, education, or economic status.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code without attacking people;
- give and receive specific, constructive feedback;
- respect different perspectives, cultures, and levels of experience;
- acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and repair harm where possible;
- credit the work and ideas of others;
- protect private information and respect clearly stated boundaries;
- prioritize the health of the project and community over personal status.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, stalking, or unwanted personal attention;
- discriminatory, demeaning, insulting, or sexualized language or imagery;
- personal or political attacks unrelated to the technical work;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- impersonation, deliberate deception, or evasion of moderation decisions;
- sustained disruption, spam, or commercial promotion unrelated to the project;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation.

## Scope

This policy applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, community events,
and other Kunlun Runtime spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the project,
including through an official account or at an event.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **kunlunengine@zixiaolabs.com** with the subject
`[CONDUCT] <short summary>`. Do not open a public issue about a conduct incident.

Include relevant links, dates, context, and any immediate safety concern. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practicable. Information will be shared only with people necessary to investigate
and respond. If a report concerns someone who can access the reporting inbox, request their recusal;
that person must not participate in handling the report.

For threats of imminent harm, contact the appropriate local emergency service first. Platform abuse
may also be reported through GitHub's reporting tools.

## Enforcement

Project moderators will consider the context, severity, impact, and pattern of behavior. Possible
responses include clarification, a private warning, content removal, a temporary participation
restriction, removal of project privileges, or a permanent ban. Serious incidents may skip earlier
steps.

Moderation decisions should be timely, proportionate, documented privately, and free from conflicts
of interest. Affected participants may request reconsideration by a maintainer who was not involved
in the original decision. Retaliation or repeated violations may result in stronger action.
